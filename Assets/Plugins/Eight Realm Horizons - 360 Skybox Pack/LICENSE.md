### Render Pipeline Compatibility

This package was authored in Unity `2022.3.62f` with URP.

The included panoramic PNG textures and skybox materials are intended for Unity skybox workflows in URP and the Built-in Render Pipeline. Unity documentation states that traditional skybox Materials and the Panoramic skybox shader are supported in the Built-in Render Pipeline and URP.

HDRP does not directly support traditional skybox Materials and uses separate sky/environment systems. HDRP users may reuse the included PNG textures as source panoramas, but HDRP-specific sky settings must be created inside the target HDRP project.
